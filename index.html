00 / The Burning Legion — architects of invasion
01

Sargeras

Titan
Burning Legion

The will behind Medivh

Possesses Medivh and uses him to open the Dark Portal with Gul’dan. Medivh’s death ends his control of the Guardian.

Background lore
Burning Legion · legacy

The lure of the tomb

Gul’dan abandons the Horde’s war to seek power in the Tomb of Sargeras. Demons within kill the warlock; Sargeras does not appear in person.

Burning Legion · legacy

The Eye of Sargeras

Illidan retrieves the Eye from the tomb and uses it to attack the Frozen Throne. Malfurion interrupts the spell before it destroys the Lich King.

Burning Legion

An unseen threat

The Legion’s founder remains absent from the launch world. His influence survives through the demons and the devastation they have caused.

02

Archimonde

Demon · man’ari eredar
——
Burning Legion

The invasion of Azeroth

Summoned by Kel’Thuzad, destroys Dalaran and leads the Legion toward Nordrassil. Malfurion’s wisps destroy him at Mount Hyjal.

Burning Legion

Defeated at Hyjal

Slain at the end of the Third War. His defeat leaves Azeroth’s survivors rebuilding after the Legion’s invasion.

03

Kil'jaeden

Demon · man’ari eredar
Burning Legion

The corruption of the orcs

Manipulates Ner’zhul and then Gul’dan to turn the orcs into the Legion’s weapons. This corruption sets the stage for the invasion of Azeroth.

Background lore
Burning Legion

Ner’zhul’s punishment

After Draenor’s destruction, captures Ner’zhul and torments his spirit. Transforms him into the Lich King to prepare another invasion of Azeroth.

Background lore
Burning Legion

Destroy the Frozen Throne

Orders Illidan to eliminate the rebellious Lich King. Renews his demand in Outland, driving Illidan’s unsuccessful assault on Icecrown.

Burning Legion

The Deceiver endures

Remains a powerful Legion leader beyond Azeroth. His confrontation with WoW adventurers has not yet occurred.

04

Mannoroth

Demon · pit lord
Burning Legion

The blood pact

Before the invasion, the orc chieftains drink his blood, binding their people to demonic fury and the Legion’s influence.

Background lore		—
Burning Legion

The curse broken

Corrupts the Warsong again, giving Grom the power to kill Cenarius. Grom later kills Mannoroth and dies in the explosion, breaking the blood curse.

Burning Legion

A defeated enslaver

Dead since his battle with Grom. The freed orcs build a new future under Thrall.

01 / The First War — founders and destroyers
05

Anduin Lothar

Human
Stormwind

The Lion of Stormwind

Defends the kingdom against the Horde and helps bring down Medivh. Leads the survivors north after Stormwind falls.

Alliance

An alliance forged

Commands the Alliance of Lordaeron against the Horde. Dies fighting Orgrim at Blackrock Mountain; Turalyon carries on his command.

—
Alliance

A fallen founder

Dead since the Second War; his legacy endures in the Alliance.

06

Llane Wrynn I

Human
Stormwind

The king falls

Rules Stormwind during the invasion. Garona assassinates him, and the kingdom falls to the Horde.

——
Stormwind

A lost king

Dead; his son Varian is now missing, leaving young Anduin as Stormwind’s king.

07

Medivh

Human · Guardian
Legion influence

The Dark Portal

Possessed by Sargeras, helps Gul’dan open the way for the Horde. Khadgar and Lothar confront and kill him.

—
Independent

The prophet returns

Warns of the Legion’s return and brings Thrall, Jaina, and the night elves toward a common defense of Mount Hyjal.

Independent

His watch has ended

Withdraws after the Legion’s defeat at Hyjal; no active role in the launch world.

08

Khadgar

Human
Kirin Tor · Stormwind

The apprentice

Uncovers his master Medivh’s corruption and helps defeat him, but is magically aged in the confrontation.

Background lore
Alliance

Beyond the portal

Destroys the Dark Portal after the Second War. On the Draenor expedition, seals the passage from the far side as the world tears apart.

—
Alliance expedition

Missing beyond Azeroth

One of the expedition’s lost heroes, commemorated in Stormwind. His later return is outside this chart.

09

Garona Halforcen

Half-orc · half-draenei
Shadow Council → divided loyalties

The unwilling assassin

Works with Khadgar against Medivh, but is compelled to murder King Llane. Her divided loyalties become a tragedy for Stormwind.

——
Independent

Out of sight

Absent from released vanilla WoW; her beta appearance at Ravenholdt was removed.

10

Gul'dan

Orc
Shadow Council · Horde · Legion

Power behind the throne

Manipulates Blackhand and works with Medivh to bring the Horde into Azeroth. Medivh’s death leaves him in a coma.

Horde → his own ambition

A fatal betrayal

Creates death knights for Orgrim, then abandons the war to seek the Tomb of Sargeras. Demons inside the tomb kill him.

Legacy

Power beyond death

Illidan consumes the power of his skull and becomes demonic; Gul’dan’s legacy shapes the war despite his death.

Legacy

Dead, but not forgotten

The original Gul’dan is dead. No alternate-timeline version belongs to this era.

11

Blackhand

Orc
Old Horde

The puppet warchief

Leads the invading Horde under Gul’dan’s influence. Orgrim kills him and takes command.

——
Old Horde

A fallen warchief

Dead since the First War; his son Rend leads the hostile Dark Horde at Blackrock Spire.

12

Orgrim Doomhammer

Orc
Old Horde

Seizing command

Overthrows Blackhand, breaks the Shadow Council’s power, and leads the Horde as Stormwind falls.

Old Horde

Victory turns to defeat

Invades the northern kingdoms and kills Lothar. Turalyon defeats him, and the Alliance captures him.

—
Horde legacy

Thrall’s predecessor

Died helping liberate captive orcs before Warcraft III. Thrall bears his hammer, and Orgrimmar bears his name.

Background lore
02 / Warcraft II — the Horde, dragons, and Draenor
13

Ner'zhul

Orc → disembodied undead spirit
—
Horde of Draenor

A world shattered

Gathers artifacts to open escape portals from Draenor. His reckless magic tears the world apart.

Scourge · breaks from Legion

The first Lich King

Commands the Scourge from the Frozen Throne and draws Arthas north. Arthas dons the helm and merges with him.

Scourge

The Frozen Throne

Part of the Lich King joined with Arthas in Northrend; no separate launch-era encounter.

14

Teron Gorefiend

Orc soul · undead human body
—
Old Horde → Horde of Draenor

The first death knight

Raised by Gul’dan in a slain human knight’s body, then serves Ner’zhul’s campaign to obtain the artifacts needed for new portals.

—
Horde of Draenor

Absent from Azeroth

No launch-world appearance. His return in Outland belongs to The Burning Crusade.

15

Grommash Hellscream

Orc
—
Warsong · Horde of Draenor

Holding the gateway

Leads the Warsong in Ner’zhul’s renewed invasion of Azeroth and helps defend the Dark Portal.

Horde → Legion → freed

The blood curse broken

Drinks Mannoroth’s blood again and kills Cenarius. Freed with Thrall’s help, kills Mannoroth at the cost of his own life.

Horde legacy

A hero remembered

Dead; remembered for freeing the orcs from Mannoroth’s blood curse.

16

Alexstrasza

Dragon · red dragonflight
—
Red dragonflight · captive

The captive Dragonqueen

Background lore		—
Red dragonflight

Free of the Dragonmaw

Freed after the Second War, she remains the Aspect of Life. She is not a launch-era questing NPC.

Background lore
17

Deathwing

Dragon · black dragonflight
—
Black dragonflight · Horde ally

An alliance of convenience

Supports the Horde of Draenor and takes black dragon eggs to Draenor, pursuing the future of his own flight.

—
Black dragonflight

The hidden patriarch

Remains out of sight while his children Onyxia and Nefarian threaten Azeroth. His return lies far beyond vanilla.

—

Background lore
Lordaeron · Alliance

Betrayed by his son

Dismisses the prophet’s warning. Arthas returns from Northrend as a death knight and murders him.

Lordaeron legacy

A kingdom in ruins

Dead; his tomb lies above the Undercity, beneath the ruins of his former capital.

Uther the Lightbringer

Human
—
Alliance · Silver Hand

The first paladins

Leads the new Knights of the Silver Hand as the Alliance’s paladins take the field against the Horde.

Alliance · Silver Hand

The mentor’s last stand

Refuses to join Arthas’s purge of Stratholme. Later dies defending Terenas’s urn from his fallen pupil.

Silver Hand legacy

The Lightbringer’s tomb

Dead; his tomb in the Western Plaguelands remains a place of remembrance.

—
Alliance expedition

Taking up the banner

Rallies the Alliance after Lothar falls, then leads its expedition to Draenor. Remains beyond the portal when it is sealed.

—
Alliance expedition

A missing hero

Unaccounted for from Azeroth’s perspective; honored by a statue in Stormwind.

Alleria Windrunner

—

The ranger beyond the portal

Alliance expedition

A missing Windrunner

Her fate is unknown to Azeroth. A Stormwind statue honors her service.

Serves as a leading commander in the Alliance’s campaign on Draenor and fights to stop Ner’zhul’s plans.

—
Alliance expedition

Lost beyond the portal

Remembered among the expedition’s missing heroes. His Outland role is revealed in a later expansion.

Fights the Horde from the skies with his gryphon Sky’ree. Joins the expedition to Draenor and is stranded beyond the portal.

—
Alliance expedition

A hero in stone

Commemorated in Stormwind alongside the other lost expedition leaders.

24

Antonidas

Human
—
Kirin Tor · Alliance

Dalaran joins the war

Pledges Dalaran’s magical strength to the Alliance against the Horde.

Background lore
Kirin Tor · Alliance

The fall of Dalaran

Jaina’s mentor stays to defend Dalaran. Arthas kills him while seizing Medivh’s spellbook for Kel’Thuzad.

Kirin Tor legacy

A city rebuilding

Dead; the surviving Kirin Tor shelter behind Dalaran’s magical barrier.

03 / Warcraft III — the fall and the rebirth
25

Arthas Menethil

Human → undead
——
Alliance → Scourge

From prince to Lich King

Purges Stratholme, claims Frostmourne, and destroys the kingdoms he once defended. Defeats Illidan at Icecrown and merges with the Lich King.

Scourge

The enemy in the north

Reigns as Lich King in Northrend while the Scourge holds much of the Plaguelands. His WoW campaign has not begun.

26

Jaina Proudmoore

Human
——
Alliance · Theramore

Choosing survival over hatred

Leads survivors west and allies with Thrall and the night elves against the Legion. Later lets the Horde defeat her father’s invasion of Durotar.

Alliance · Theramore

An uneasy peace

Rules Theramore and maintains fragile ties with Thrall despite growing Alliance–Horde tensions.

27

Muradin Bronzebeard

Dwarf · Bronzebeard
——
Alliance · Explorers’ League

The price of Frostmourne

Helps Arthas in Northrend but warns against taking the cursed blade. Is struck down when Arthas frees it and is believed dead.

Alliance

Presumed dead

His brothers mourn him. His actual fate is not revealed in vanilla WoW.

28

Thrall

Orc
——
New Horde

A new home for the Horde

Leads the orcs to Kalimdor, allies with trolls and tauren, and helps defend Hyjal. Establishes Durotar and Orgrimmar.

Horde

Warchief in Orgrimmar

Leads the new Horde, now also allied with Sylvanas’s Forsaken. Seeks security for his people amid mounting conflict.

29

Cairne Bloodhoof

Tauren
——
Tauren → New Horde

An alliance for survival

Thrall helps his people escape the centaur. Cairne joins the Horde and helps defend its new homeland.

Horde · tauren

High chieftain

Leads the united tauren from Thunder Bluff in Mulgore and remains a close ally of Thrall.

30

Vol'jin

Troll · Darkspear
——
Darkspear · New Horde

The Darkspear’s new path

Succeeds Sen’jin as leader of the Darkspear. Helps Thrall and Rexxar defend the Horde’s settlements in Kalimdor.

Horde · Darkspear

Exiles of the Echo Isles

Advises Thrall in Orgrimmar while his people have been driven from the Echo Isles by Zalazane.

31

Rexxar

Mok’nathal · half-orc, half-ogre
——
New Horde · Stonemaul

Champion of the Horde

Unites allies in defense of Durotar and leads the fight against Admiral Daelin Proudmoore’s forces.

Horde

The wandering champion

Travels western Kalimdor with Misha. Horde adventurers seek his aid on the path to confronting Onyxia.

32

Sylvanas Windrunner

High elf → undead
——
Quel’Thalas → Scourge → Forsaken

The Banshee Queen

Arthas kills and raises her during the invasion of Quel’Thalas. She breaks free of the Lich King and seizes Lordaeron with the Forsaken.

Forsaken · Horde

Queen of the Undercity

Rules the free undead beneath Lordaeron’s ruins. Her alliance with the Horde is pragmatic and uneasy.

33

Kel'Thuzad

Human → lich
——
Cult of the Damned · Scourge

Architect of the plague

Killed by Arthas, then resurrected by him as a lich using the Sunwell. Summons Archimonde and later holds the Scourge’s lands for Arthas.

Scourge

The Lich King’s lieutenant

A leading power behind the Scourge in Lordaeron. The Naxxramas raid arrives in a later vanilla patch, outside this cutoff.

34

Tyrande Whisperwind

Night elf
——
Sentinels · night elves

The defense of Kalimdor

Awakens Malfurion and frees Illidan to resist the Legion. Joins the defense of Hyjal and later helps the blood elf survivors.

Alliance · night elves

High priestess of Elune

Leads her people from Darnassus. The night elves have joined the Alliance, and Malfurion is missing in the Emerald Dream.

35

Malfurion Stormrage

Night elf
——
Night elves · druids

The sacrifice at Hyjal

Awakens to confront the Legion and calls the wisps that destroy Archimonde. Later stops Illidan’s spell against the Frozen Throne.

Cenarion Circle · night elves

Lost in the Dream

Trapped in the Emerald Dream; Fandral Staghelm leads the night elf druids in his absence.

36

Illidan Stormrage

Night elf → demon
——
Night elves → his own forces

The Betrayer’s ascent

Consumes the Skull of Gul’dan, conquers Outland, and serves Kil’jaeden’s demand to destroy the Lich King. Arthas defeats him at Icecrown.

Illidari

Beyond the launch world

Has returned to Outland after his defeat. His confrontation with WoW adventurers belongs to The Burning Crusade.

37

Kael'thas Sunstrider

High elf / blood elf
——
Alliance remnants → Illidan

A prince without a kingdom

Leads the blood elf survivors, escapes Garithos with naga aid, and joins Illidan in Outland. Helps him attack Icecrown.

Blood elves · Illidan’s forces

A people awaiting salvation

Remains in Outland with Illidan. The blood elves have not yet joined the Horde.

38

Cenarius

Demigod · keeper of the grove
——
Night elves · protectors of nature

The forest strikes back

Confronts the Warsong’s destruction of Ashenvale. Grom and his newly corrupted warriors kill him after drinking Mannoroth’s blood.

Cenarion legacy

The forest remembers

Dead in the physical world; his teachings endure among the druids of the Cenarion Circle. His return lies beyond vanilla.

39

Maiev Shadowsong

Night elf
——
Watchers

The relentless jailer

Pursues Illidan from Kalimdor to Outland and briefly captures him. Lady Vashj and Kael’thas free him, forcing her surviving Watchers to retreat.

Watchers

The hunt beyond Azeroth

Last seen pursuing Illidan in Outland. She has no appearance in the original WoW launch world.

40

Lady Vashj

Naga · formerly Highborne
——
Naga · Illidan’s forces

The sea witch’s alliance

Answers Illidan’s call and brings Kael’thas’s blood elves to his cause. Helps conquer Outland and joins the attack on the Frozen Throne.

Naga · Illidan’s forces

Beyond the Dark Portal

Remains allied with Illidan in Outland. Her WoW storyline is outside the launch-era setting.

41

Daelin Proudmoore

Human
—
Kul Tiras · Alliance

Grand Admiral

Leads the Alliance navy against the Horde during the Second War, making Kul Tiras a major force in the struggle at sea.

Background lore
Kul Tiras · Alliance

A war Jaina will not fight

Seizes control of Theramore and attacks Thrall’s new Horde. Jaina allows Rexxar and the Horde to defeat him; Daelin dies refusing peace.

Kul Tiras · legacy

A father’s legacy

Dead after the Durotar campaign. Jaina rules Theramore, carrying the consequences of choosing peace over her father’s war.

04 / World of Warcraft — the kingdoms left behind
42

Magni Bronzebeard

Dwarf · Bronzebeard
—
Ironforge · Alliance

The king under siege

Defends Ironforge against the Horde and supports the Alliance’s war.

Background lore		—
Alliance · Ironforge

King under the mountain

43

Varian Wrynn

Human
Stormwind

The surviving prince

Loses his father Llane and escapes the fall of Stormwind with Lothar’s survivors.

Background lore		——
Alliance · Stormwind

The missing king
